PLAYER USE
Research Coin Maker after Automation 2. Base cost: 500 red and 500 green science,
60 seconds per research unit; the existing research-difficulty setting still
scales research cost. Build it using 50 steel plates, 200 iron gears, 50 green
circuits and one assembling machine 2. Supply electricity and saleable goods
using inserters. Extract coins from its output using another inserter.
No recipe selection is needed or available. Each complete normal-quality batch
is sold independently, at the current Market Sell price. The listed input goods
are alternatives, not a requirement to supply all of them. Incomplete batches
stay inside. The machine rotates through available goods to avoid starvation.
Normal-quality base rate is one transaction every two seconds. Building quality
and accepted modules use native speed bonuses. There are always four module slots.

POWER AND MODULES
500 kW constant drain + 1.5 MW active component = 2 MW total without modules.
Only speed and efficiency module categories are allowed, including beacon
transmission. Productivity and quality modules do not contribute, even in a
mixed beacon. Fixed drain is not modified. Native bonuses, beacon transmission,
module quality and efficiency limits come from the engine, not copied constants.

IMPLEMENTATION AND LIMITS
Only registered Coin Makers are visited every six ticks. There is no recurring
full-surface scan. A prototype-filtered discovery occurs on init/configuration.
Inputs stay in native inventories until a completed transaction. No hidden
storage entity or item escrow is used; save state holds work progress only.
The fixed internal recipe provides native filtered inventories and is disabled
for native crafting so it cannot consume all goods together. Sales use a custom
status and progress display. Consequently, native crafting animation and native
'read working'/'recipe finished' circuit signals do not represent the scripted
sales. Circuit enable/disable was tested; other circuit integrations are not
claimed as fully tested.
Headless cannot visually inspect the gold tint, icon rendering, research-tree
layout or GUI. These remain graphical-client playtest items. No two-client
multiplayer session, large-factory performance benchmark or broad third-party
mod matrix was run. Per-force behaviour and a local server save were tested.
Existing enemy-expansion collision-mask warnings appear in the Space Age QA
logs; they also predate this change. There were no new startup/runtime errors
in the completed test runs.
