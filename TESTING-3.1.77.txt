CONTENTS
- Industrial Loader graphics and icons in yellow, red, blue and green, preserving the existing three/four-tier condition, all directions and input/output states.
- Wind Turbine graphics, rotation and icons. Native 20-frame timing, 300x175 frame size, scale, shift, power, research and enable/disable behaviour are preserved.
- Separate Cave Accumulator (3x3): Normal quality 50 MJ, 3 MW input/output, 100 seconds; 50 batteries, 10 steel plates, 10 advanced circuits, 20 electronic circuits.
- Separate Cave Accumulator (6x6): Normal quality 100 MJ, 6 MW input/output, 200 seconds; 100 batteries, 20 steel plates, 20 advanced circuits, 40 electronic circuits.
- Both unlock with the existing Electric Energy Accumulators research. Migration handles existing forces independently. Vanilla and market MK accumulators are not replaced.
- Native accumulator energy priority, quality handling, sounds, health, circuit output signal and wire range are inherited. Lights animate only while the native engine charges or discharges; no new per-tick script is installed.
- Industrial chest fixes from 3.1.76 and all unrelated systems are retained unchanged.

TEST IN FACTORIO
This build has not been loaded in the Factorio engine here. Static and focused Lua checks do not replace an in-game test.
1. Load a copy of the existing 3.1.76 save. A force that completed Electric Energy Accumulators should see both new recipes; a force without that research should not.
2. Place each loader tier facing all four directions; switch input/output, check arrows, filters, animated belt visibility and clearance next to chests/inserters.
3. Check existing and newly placed wind turbines: alignment, rotating blades, shadow, power and enable/disable setting.
4. Place both accumulator sizes beside belts/inserters and each other. Verify 3x3/6x6 selection and placement, surplus charging, deficit discharging, full/empty states and circuit signal A.
5. If using Quality, check a higher-quality accumulator and save/reload once. Quality scaling is provided by the native accumulator engine.
6. Test recipe unlocks separately for multiplayer forces if the server uses more than one force.
