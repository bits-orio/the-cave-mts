The Cave 3.1.81 - Industrial Loader east/west size correction

Scope
This build corrects the size and position of the approved left/right loader
artwork. The original stable 3.1.76 sprites were used as the sizing reference.
The 3.1.80 packer had fitted the artwork by its width, reducing the body height
to about 110 pixels and leaving its lower edge too high over the belt.

The corrected body is 120x149 source pixels in both side directions. Its
position matches the original sprite, with body bounds (25,6)-(145,155) for
the east slot and (26,6)-(146,155) for the west slot. These are projected
graphic dimensions, not changes to the entity's 1x1 placement footprint.
The PNG atlases remain 848x340, with 212x170 cells, scale 0.25 and the original
shift. The approved four-corner design, metal rails, colours and arrows remain.
The lower side-view rails now extend to the original sprite's lower edge.

Added: TESTING-3.1.81.txt

Verification performed
- All 16 east/west cells match the original housing bounds and origin.
- All 16 north/south cells are pixel-identical to 3.1.80 and 3.1.78.
- All colours and both flow states share the same side-view silhouette.
- Both belt openings remain transparent; no body or shadow touches a frame edge.
- Every Lua file, setting, recipe, speed, filter and entity identity is unchanged.
- Loader prototype equality checked in a Lua harness with three tiers and
  with the optional fourth tier; sprite references checked against the PNGs.
- ZIP CRC and all packaged file bytes checked against the working copy.

In-game test still required
Factorio was not run in this environment. Enable 3.1.81 and test the loaders
in the same setup shown in your screenshots. Inspect both side directions,
especially the lower rim over the belt. Check yellow, red and blue loaders,
and the green tier when available with Space Age, in both input/output modes.
Rotate through all four directions to compare the unchanged north/south views.
Check that items still load and unload with the original belt speeds.
