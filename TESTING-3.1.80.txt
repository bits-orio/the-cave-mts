The Cave 3.1.80 - locked Industrial Loader side-view design
Date: 2026-09-18

Baseline
Built directly from the verified 3.1.78 ZIP with the approved wind turbine.
No files or artwork from the rejected 3.1.79 side-view design are included.

Changes
- The left/right housing uses the final user-approved preview derived from
  the front housing: exactly four separate painted corner pads, interrupted
  rails, the original panel arrangement and an open sideways mouth.
- Yellow, red, cyan-blue and lime-green variants are present. Generated paint
  variants are applied through masks on the locked source, preserving its
  silhouette, opening and neutral body pixels outside the painted areas.
- Both input and output states use identical bodies; only the arrow-panel
  pixels differ between states. No new vents, feet or corner blocks are added.
- Side-view shadows follow the actual new silhouette.

Preserved
- All 16 north/south sprite cells remain pixel-identical to 3.1.78.
- Icons, all Lua code, speeds, recipes, filters, research, settings,
  migrations, runtime logic and existing-save entity identities are unchanged.
- 848x340 RGBA atlases, 212x170 frames, scale 0.25 and shift
  (0.15625,0.0703125) remain unchanged. No prototype file is edited.
- The 1x1 footprint and collision/selection boxes are unchanged. The new
  side housing is uniformly scaled without stretching, fits within the old
  opaque side-body bounds, and stays within the placement square. Its open
  tunnel is aligned to the existing belt centre. Shadows may extend past the
  placement square, like the existing north/south shadows.
- Approved wind turbine, accumulators, chests and other systems unchanged.
